## Choose the right workflow

Use the OCR workbench when a PDF page is an image and its words cannot be selected or copied. Typical examples include archived research papers, signed supplier documents, scanned manuals, and image-only reports. If the PDF already contains clean selectable text, start with the [regular PDF translator](/translate) instead; it avoids an unnecessary recognition step.

## Before you start

- Sign in before uploading or processing a file.
- Choose a source language that matches the document and one of the supported target languages.
- Remove a password or access restriction before upload if you are authorized to do so.
- Use the clearest available scan. Straight pages, readable contrast, and complete margins improve recognition.

## Step-by-step OCR translation

1. Open the [OCR PDF translator](/ocrtranslator) and upload the scanned PDF.
2. Confirm the source and target languages. You can limit processing to the pages you actually need.
3. Start the task. OCR detects page regions, text blocks, reading order, and tables before translation.
4. Compare the original page with the workbench result. Correct names, numbers, headings, and misplaced blocks.
5. Translate the reviewed content and inspect the final pages.
6. Generate PDF, Markdown, or HTML and check the downloaded file before sharing it.

While a document or task is active, the upload control is disabled to prevent the workbench from replacing the current context. When the regular translator detects a scan with high confidence, it can guide you to this OCR workflow.

## What to review carefully

- **Names and identifiers:** people, organizations, invoice numbers, product codes, and references.
- **Numbers and units:** dates, prices, percentages, measurements, and decimal separators.
- **Page structure:** columns, captions, footnotes, headers, and reading order.
- **Visual content:** tables, stamps, signatures, handwriting, and text close to page edges.

OCR and translation assist with document review; they do not replace comparison with the source. Legal, academic, medical, and financial material should receive a qualified human review.

## Export options

- **PDF** for a page-oriented document that follows the reviewed workbench layout where technically possible.
- **Markdown** for editable text, documentation, or downstream publishing workflows.
- **HTML** for browser-based review or reuse in a web content workflow.

Complex tables, unusual fonts, low-resolution images, and handwriting may not reproduce perfectly in any export. Keep the source file available for final verification.

## Account, storage, and deletion

Processing requires an account, and tasks are separated by user. Documents and related stored objects are normally scheduled for deletion after about seven days, subject to the [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) and legal exceptions. Do not upload a file unless you have the right to process its contents.
